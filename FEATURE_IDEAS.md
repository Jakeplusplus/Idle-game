# Dragon Hoard Feature Ideas & Brainstorming

Consider this document a living scratchpad for the Dragon Hoard Idle Game. Any ideas for future mechanics, balance tweaks, or content expansions that are not part of the active implementation plan should be logged here.

## 📝 Proposed Features

- **Moving Mountains**: Shift your hoard to a stronger strategic location.
- **Scouting Towns**: Discover new settlements to raid or demand tribute from.
- **Forging Your Own Treasure**: A crafting system to combine raw materials into unique artifacts.
- **Hiring Adventurers**: Employ humans to raid other dungeons on your behalf.
- **Mountain Upgrades & Buildings**: Construct specialized facilities and upgrades to significantly boost the gathering efficiency of your minions.
  - _Treasure Vault_: A specific building to serve as an inventory system to hold and display found treasures.
- **Gold Capacity Limit**: Introduce a hard cap on maximum hoard size. The dragon must expand their lair (by having Kobold Miners dig deeper) to increase the maximum gold capacity.
- **Dynastic Prestige Mechanics**: Hatchlings eventually move out to settle entirely new Mountains, allowing you to save multiple Dragons and Mountains across the generational timeline.
